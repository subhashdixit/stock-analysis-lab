How this works · scope and limitations

Compare last-price, average-drift and autoregressive (AR5 ridge) methods. A 60% training / 20% validation / 20% test split preserves time order. Choose a method using validation MAE, then evaluate once on the held-out test. Future steps mean observations, not calendar dates. This research tool gives no trading advice, current market feed or guaranteed returns.
